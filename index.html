Race day, step by step

From your start to the result.

  1. Step 01

    Get your start

    Fair starts use your recent 5K pace to set your own start time. In Same start, everyone starts together.

  2. Step 02

    Countdown

    At the route start, check location and recording readiness. The countdown runs to your start, not the first runner's.

  3. Step 03

    Start line

    Follow your personal start time. When your start opens, start recording at the line and run.

  4. Step 04

    The chase

    Race the shared course. Course positions and gaps are estimates, with stale or missing data called out.

  5. Step 05

    Review and result

    Recorded is not always final. Catchfire shows review status before a result is finalized. Then share it or propose a rematch.

Straight answers

Before your first run.

More on recording, results and accounts in Support.

Who can join?

Catchfire is in free beta on iPhone and Android, by invitation. Request access for your device and we will send the testing instructions.

Do I need a pace?

No pace is needed for Just run or an unrestricted Same start race. Fair start and pace-limited races require a recent 5K pace.

What is a fair start?

A pace-adjusted personal start, set from your recent 5K pace. It is not a prediction and does not promise equal finishes. First across the line wins.

What kind of race is this?

Free, unofficial running races. Catchfire races are not official events and are not sanctioned by any city, park authority, road authority, federation, or governing body unless expressly stated in writing.

Does it sync with Strava or Garmin?

No. There is no automatic sync. Export a finished race as a FIT or GPX file and upload it yourself, for example to Strava or Garmin Connect.

Where does my data go?

Just runs stay on your phone. During a race, Catchfire uses location for route progress and finish detection. The privacy policy has the detail.
