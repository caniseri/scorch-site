Mapped routes for Scorch races.

These 5K+ routes come from familiar running corridors with clear paths, recognizable landmarks, and strong local runner density. They are candidates for Scheduled races, private Anytime runs, and the planned Weekly Challenge.

Route standard

Pilot routes work best on clear, familiar public routes with suitable start and finish areas, stable GPS visibility, and minimal crossing complexity. Route listings are not guarantees of safety, permits, or supervision.
